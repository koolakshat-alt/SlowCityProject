# Standardized Export & Render Naming Conventions

## Format Syntax
`[PROJECT]_[EPISODE]_[STAGE]_[CUT_VERSION]_[RESOLUTION]_[DATE].[ext]`

---

## Token Reference
- **PROJECT:** `SCP` (The Slow City Project)
- **EPISODE:** `EP01`, `EP02`, etc.
- **STAGE:**
  - `ROUGH`: Assembly / Rough Cut
  - `REVIEW`: Work-In-Progress for internal review or client notes
  - `MASTER`: Color-graded, mixed, final broadcast-ready export
- **CUT_VERSION:** `v01`, `v02`, `v03`, etc.
- **RESOLUTION:** `4K` (3840x2160) or `1080p` (1920x1080)
- **DATE:** `YYYYMMDD`

---

## Examples
- Rough Cut: `SCP_EP01_ROUGH_v01_1080p_20261029.mp4`
- Review Cut: `SCP_EP01_REVIEW_v02_1080p_20261102.mp4`
- Final Master: `SCP_EP01_MASTER_v01_4K_20261110.mp4`
