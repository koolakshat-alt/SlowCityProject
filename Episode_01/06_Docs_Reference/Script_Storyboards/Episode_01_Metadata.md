# Episode 01: Slow Heritage — Script & Production Notes

## Metadata
- **Episode Number:** EP01
- **Working Title:** Slow Heritage: The Rhythm of Forgotten Pace
- **Target Runtime:** 11:00
- **Audio Target:** -14 LUFS master, dialogue peaked at -6 dBFS

---

## Full Script Draft

### [00:00 - 01:30] ACT I: THE OPENING NOTE
**Visual:** Slow creeping tracking shot down an empty morning alleyway. Architectural arches framing mist.  
**Audio:** Low drone, distant ambient chimes, isolated footsteps.  
**Voiceover:**  
> "Every modern city moves at a tempo it didn't choose. We measure our days by latency, notifications, and transit times—as if acceleration were the only metric of growth.  
> But beneath the asphalt and modern glass, some places still remember how to breathe. This is not about nostalgia. It is about understanding what we sacrificed in exchange for speed."

---

### [01:30 - 04:30] ACT II: THE MORNING TEXTURE
**Visual:** Boiling brass kettles, steam catching early sunlight, shutter gates unlocking, morning bread deliveries.  
**Audio:** Foley-rich natural audio. Soft warm acoustic guitar.  
**Voiceover:**  
> "Before the engine hum takes over, the city wakes up with physical touch. There is no urgency in how the kettle boils, or how dough is kneaded. The morning routine here is not an optimization problem—it is a conversation."

---

### [04:30 - 07:30] ACT III: STONE AND SILENCE
**Visual:** Symmetrical architectural arches, light cutting through lattice windows, dust motes in sunbeams.  
**Audio:** Hallway reverberation, subtle synth pad.  
**Voiceover:**  
> "Architecture is not just static stone; it is frozen tempo. High ceilings and wide verandas were built to cool the air, but they also cool the mind. When you stand inside a room designed three hundred years ago, your pulse instinctively slows down to match the room."

---

### [07:30 - 09:30] ACT IV: THE CRAFTSMAN'S HANDS
**Visual:** Macro detail of hands carving wood, polishing metal, or weaving fabric. Eye-level portraits.  
**Audio:** Tactile sound design. Rhythmic scrapes and cuts.  
**Voiceover:**  
> "Speed produces commodities. Slowness produces character. When an artisan spends twelve hours on an object that could be stamped out in seconds by a machine, they aren't being inefficient. They are leaving an imprint of their mortality onto matter."

---

### [09:30 - 11:30] ACT V: TWILIGHT RESOLUTION
**Visual:** Sun setting behind terracotta rooflines. Streetlamps buzzing to life. Distant wide drone silhouette.  
**Audio:** Score builds to emotional resolution, then fades out into soft evening ambience.  
**Voiceover:**  
> "To choose a slower pace is not an escape from reality. It is a return to it.  
> Subscribe to join us on the next journey through the old quarter."
